# RCAEval RE2-OB/TT: data evidence and suitability

## Judgment

**This is not an arbitrary custom dataset.** RCAEval is a published, author-maintained benchmark, and RE2-OB and RE2-TT are explicitly named benchmark subsets. Our held corpus is therefore literature-backed at the benchmark/subset level. It is suitable for a reproducible controlled-fault RCA evaluation, provided the report names the exact acquired release and preprocessing. It does **not**, on present evidence, establish exact-release fidelity to the original data or representativeness of production incidents.

## Evidence and scope

- **Publication:** [RCAEval, DOI 10.1145/3701716.3715290](https://doi.org/10.1145/3701716.3715290), *Companion Proceedings of the ACM on Web Conference 2025*, pp. 777–780. The paper introduces a telemetry RCA benchmark; this is a WWW Companion publication, not a claim that the dataset is a long-form WWW main-conference paper. [Paper record](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3701716.3715290).
- **Author provenance:** [Author repository](https://github.com/phamquiluan/RCAEval), “Available Datasets” / “File Structure,” explicitly recommends the [Hugging Face dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/phamquiluan/RCAEval) and links Zenodo as another distribution. The card’s “Datasets,” “Case index,” and “Layout” sections enumerate RE2-OB and RE2-TT separately: 90 cases each, Online Boutique and Train Ticket respectively, with metrics/logs/traces (except known individual missing data). The local catalog records 180 held cases total, 90+90.
- **Benchmark name is not release identity:** Hugging Face’s current card says its Parquet is a conversion of original `metrics.json`, `logs.csv`, and `traces.csv`; the repository claims value-for-value conversion, while the original archives remain on [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/14590730). These are distinct formats/distributions. Our `SOURCES.md` pins the acquired Parquet snapshot to commit `afeacb11bcc94dadfd1c8f483ee4377b2b8b614e`. That pin establishes which HF snapshot we acquired, not that the 180-case local selection, files, transformations, or exclusions are identical to an original paper experiment. Full historical CSV/JSON-to-Parquet comparison was not performed here. Local catalog caveats include two incomplete-modality cases, one case missing logs, and 12 cases with all-null metric columns; analysis handling should be stated.
- **One subsequent published/accepted study:** TORAI is accepted at FSE 2026 ([ACM DOI 10.1145/3808137](https://doi.org/10.1145/3808137); [paper, §4.1 and §7](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.13522v1)). It describes 270 cases from Online Boutique, Sock Shop, and Train Ticket. Crucially, §7 explicitly says TORAI was integrated into RCAEval, and separately links its experimental datasets on Figshare. This verifies a research/software connection to RCAEval. Matching application names and counts alone do **not** prove reuse of the same telemetry dataset. TORAI's use of our exact pinned Parquet release or our exact 180-case subset remains unverified.
- **Ops-lite is not corroboration yet:** `data_catalog.md` records 455 locally described ops-lite cases versus the OpenRCA 2.0 paper’s 500, with no confirmed provenance link between them. Treat ops-lite’s claimed injection procedure as distributor-stated, not verified identity with the paper release or independent truth validation. It is not primary evidence for this RCAEval judgment.

## Suitability in plain language

Controlled fault injection is legitimate benchmark evidence: known injected faults enable repeatable comparisons and clear labels. But it samples particular systems, injected fault types, telemetry setups, and repetitions; it cannot alone show that a method generalizes to messy production failures. Use the held RE2-OB/TT for **benchmark performance**, not as proof of production readiness. Avoid calling the subset an exact reproduction until the pinned snapshot, selected cases, preprocessing, and exclusions are matched to the target protocol.
